% --- Thesis abstract.
%     For German thesis also provide an English version via the optional
%     argument: \abstract[English]{German}
\abstract{
Forward error correction (FEC) for packet networks operates on fixed-size generations of
packets belonging to the same flow, rather than on packets individually as they arrive
interleaved across many concurrent flows. This thesis presents the design and
simulation-based verification of a packet buffer that bridges this mismatch, implemented as an
extension to the Open NIC Shell targeting an AMD/Xilinx Alveo U55C. Following a shared-memory
architecture, packet payloads are stored in a single HBM-backed pool shared across all flows,
while each flow is tracked through a small queue of packet addresses that a packet reader stage
drains in complete generations for the encoder. The design was verified with an almost
UVM-like testbench and, across a range of packet sizes, flow-interleaving patterns, and
configurations, sustains throughput close to 100 Gb/s while keeping its own contribution to
packet latency under 10 microseconds. The results also show that overall latency is largely
governed by how traffic is interleaved across flows rather than by the buffer itself, and point
to flip-flop-heavy resource usage and fixed, elaboration-time configuration as the main
directions for future work.}
