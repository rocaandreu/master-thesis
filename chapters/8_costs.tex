\chapter{Cost breakdown}\label{ch:cost}

This chapter estimates the cost of carrying out this thesis. Costs are grouped into four
categories, following standard project-costing practice: personnel, equipment, consumables,
and indirect costs. Equipment normally provided by the host institution and used at no
incremental cost to this specific project is noted as such rather than charged in full, since
it is not a cost this project actually incurred. Indirect costs (electricity, water,
cleaning, general facility overhead, and similar costs that cannot be attributed to a single
line item) are estimated as 5\,\% of the sum of the other three categories.

\section{Personnel costs}

The work was carried out by a single engineer over a period of six
months, at a dedication of six to eight hours per day. Assuming 20 working days per month, this
corresponds to between 720 and 960 hours, or 840 hours at the midpoint of that range, which is
used as the basis for the estimate below. \\

The hourly rate used is representative of a junior engineer's gross annual salary in Spain,
taken as  35{,}000 \euro/year. Divided over a standard working year of roughly 1{,}720 hours,
this corresponds to an hourly rate of about  20.3 \euro/h, which is rounded to  20 \euro/h for the
estimate below.

\section{Equipment and software costs}

The development workstation and all software tools used were provided by the host university as part of its existing
infrastructure and institutional academic licenses, and were used at no incremental cost to
this project. The target Alveo U55C accelerator card was not used in this work, since the
design was verified in simulation only (Section~\ref{subsec:assumptions}), and is therefore not
included in the budget either. Equipment and software costs charged to this project are
accordingly 0 \euro.

\section{Consumables}

As this thesis did not involve physical prototyping or hardware assembly, consumable costs are 
limited to minor documentation expenses (printing and binding of this document), estimated at 
50 \euro.

\section{Indirect costs}

Indirect costs are estimated at 5\,\% of the sum of the personnel, equipment/software, and
consumables subtotals, i.e. 5\,\% of  16{,}850 \euro, giving  842.50 \euro.
